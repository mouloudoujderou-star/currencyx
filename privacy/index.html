Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

CurrencyX ("we") is an app by an independent developer. Questions about privacy: oujderrou@icloud.com.

What stays on your phone

Your converter list, settings, rate alerts, trips and expenses are stored on your device. We don't receive them.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not sell your data.

Ads (AdMob) and ads measurement (TikTok)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. We also advertise CurrencyX on TikTok and use the TikTok Business SDK to learn which ads bring people to the app (events such as first conversion, first expense, trial started and subscription bought, with price and currency). These SDKs receive device information such as model, OS version and IP address.

iOS asks your permission first ("Allow tracking"). If you allow it, Google and TikTok may receive your device's advertising identifier for personalized ads and measurement. If you decline, they don't, and ads are not personalized with it. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See Google's privacy policy and TikTok's privacy policy.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: anonymous accounts, limits and our server, in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Anthropic: the AI that reads photos you choose to scan and answers spending questions (PRO). Only the photo or question is sent, through our server. Not used to train AI models under our API terms.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: subscriptions and payments. We never see your card details.
  • Google AdMob and TikTok: ads and ads measurement, as described above.
  • Exchange rate providers (currency-api via jsDelivr/Cloudflare, Frankfurter): the app downloads public rates. These servers receive your IP address like any website.

How long we keep data

Usage counts: 24 months. Anonymous account and purchase status: while the account is active. Scanned photos: not stored. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. You can ask us to delete your server data at oujderrou@icloud.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR), you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to processing. Write to oujderrou@icloud.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes.
