Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › CurrencyX › Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

I paid but PRO is not unlocked.

Open CurrencyX › Settings › CurrencyX PRO › Restore, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The converter uses the last downloaded rates and your journal is on your phone. Scanning and live rates need internet.

Why is the booth rate different from the app?

The app shows the mid-market rate. Booths, banks and cards add a margin. Use Fee check to see how much it costs you.

A scan read the wrong amount.

You can edit the amount, currency and category before saving. A sharp, close photo with good light works best.

How do I change the language?

Settings › Language opens the iPhone settings for CurrencyX. 12 languages: English, French, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Turkish, Indonesian, Malay, Japanese and Korean.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything on your phone. To delete the anonymous account and usage data on our servers, email us and we will do it within 30 days.
